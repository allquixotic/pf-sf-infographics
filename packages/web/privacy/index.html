Privacy policy

Last updated October 4, 2026

How this site uses data

pf-sf-infographics runs in your browser. There is no application backend, account system, or server-side database. GitHub Pages serves the site's pages and files. We have not designed the app to collect or log data about you, and its developers have added no cookies, analytics, or tracking features.

Your settings and chosen content source are saved on your device using browser storage. Artwork and ZIP files you add stay in this browser too. The app processes them and renders your infographics on your device, without uploading them to us.

Saved data stays until you remove it or your browser clears it. Use "Forget supplied art" under Artwork to remove saved images and ZIPs. To remove all saved settings and artwork, clear this site's data in your browser settings. Files you download or print are separate copies under your control.

GitHub Pages logs visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in its hosting documentation. We do not control the HTTP headers GitHub Pages sends, including any headers that set cookies, or any tracking GitHub may use. GitHub's handling of data, retention, and international transfers is covered by the GitHub Privacy Statement.

If you choose a custom content URL or GitHub repository, your browser requests files from that host. The host receives your IP address and ordinary request information. Its privacy policy applies, as do the policies of other sites when you follow their links.

Your data protection rights

We use the data kept in your browser only to remember your choices and generate the output you request. We do not receive a copy, keep server-side user records, sell your data, or use it for profiling or automated decisions about you. We cannot access or delete the data on your device. You can remove it as described above.

Where the GDPR applies, you have rights to access, correct, or erase personal data, restrict or object to its use, and receive a portable copy, subject to the law's conditions. If processing relies on consent, you can withdraw that consent. You can also complain to your local data protection authority. For data GitHub holds, use the privacy contact options in its statement.

For questions about this app, contact its maintainers through the GitHub repository. Issues are public, so please do not include private information. Messages you choose to post there are separate from using this app and are handled through GitHub.
