## Notes

- **Pathfinder:** Impossible Magic (July 2026) remastered the magus and summoner and added the necromancer and runesmith. Dark Archive (Remastered) (February 2026) covers the psychic and thaumaturge. Guns & Gears (Remastered) (February 2025) covers the gunslinger and inventor.
- **Pathfinder playtests:** the daredevil and slayer come from the *Risks & Rewards* playtest (February 17 – April 10, 2026) and are slated for *Tome of Dungeons* (Gen Con 2027). No other class playtest ran in 2026.
- **Starfinder:** *Tech Core* goes on sale October 7, 2026 with the final mechanic and technomancer. Until then this project shows the 2025 Tech Class Playtest versions and marks them as playtest; update `status`, `source` and any changed numbers when the book is out.
- **Starfinder playtest:** the luminary playtest ran May 11 – July 10, 2026; no book has been announced.
- Pathfinder 2e classes are compatible with Starfinder 2e (Paizo calls this *anachronism*), but each infographic shows only its own game's classes.
- Ratings are this project's estimates, not official data. Corrections are welcome; see [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
